PilotDECODER is my personal aviation learning tool. I'm Dr. Zane Wubbena, currently the Managing Director of Forecasting in School Finance at the Texas Education Agency. But I like airplanes, aviation, and learning.

Pilot Tools

  • METAR Decoder

    Any METAR (Meteorological Aerodrome Report), decoded into plain-English weather.

  • Density Altitude Decoder

    Density altitude worked line by line, the way the FAA written test does it.

  • Wind Tunnel Decoder

    Watch air flow over a wing and then change the angle of attack (AOA), see lift, drag, and stall live.

  • Flight Pattern Decoder

    The VFR traffic pattern, animated: circuits, entries, radio calls, and live wind at Georgetown Executive (KGTU).

  • Zulu Decoder

    Convert between Zulu (UTC) and the four U.S. time zones, in 24-hour and 12-hour time.

  • Cloud Decoder

    Upload a photo of the sky and get the cloud type(s) identified, from a pilot's perspective.

Resources

Certification, medical & airman records

  • Integrated Airman Certification and Rating Application (IACRA) — Apply for student pilot certificates, airman certificates, ratings, and other certification actions.
  • FAA MedXPress — Complete the online application for an FAA medical certificate before visiting an Aviation Medical Examiner.
  • Aviation Medical Examiner Locator — Find an FAA-designated Aviation Medical Examiner.
  • FAA Airmen Inquiry — Look up airman information and access FAA airman-record services.
  • FCC Universal Licensing System — Apply for and manage FCC radio licenses, including a Restricted Radiotelephone Operator Permit when required.

Weather & flight planning

  • Aviation Weather Center — Access METARs, TAFs, PIREPs, AIRMETs, SIGMETs, graphical forecasts, radar, satellite imagery, and other aviation weather products.
  • Leidos Flight Service (1-800-WX-BRIEF) — Obtain official weather briefings and file, amend, activate, or close flight plans.
  • SkyVector — View interactive VFR and IFR charts and use chart-based flight-planning tools.

Airports, NOTAMs & airspace

  • FAA Airport Data and Information Portal (ADIP) — Search official airport, runway, frequency, facility, ownership, and field-elevation data.
    • ADIP — Lockhart Municipal Airport (50R)
    • ADIP — Georgetown Executive Airport at Johnny Gantt Field (KGTU)
    • ADIP — Austin–Bergstrom International Airport (KAUS)
    • ADIP — San Marcos Regional Airport (KHYI)
  • FAA Airport Diagrams — Search and download official FAA airport diagrams by airport identifier.
  • FAA NOTAM Search — Search current Notices to Air Missions. Enter 50R for Lockhart Municipal Airport.
  • FAA Temporary Flight Restriction List — Review Temporary Flight Restrictions currently published by the FAA.
  • FAA VFR Raster Charts — Download official FAA sectional charts, terminal area charts, and other VFR chart products.
  • VFRMap — Pan and zoom the VFR sectional chart in your browser; search an identifier such as KGTU to jump to an airport.
  • FAA Chart Supplement — Download the Chart Supplement (formerly the Airport/Facility Directory) with airport, frequency, runway, and service details.
  • Terminal Area Forecast (TAF) — Query the FAA's historical and forecast aviation-activity data for U.S. airports (airport planning; distinct from the weather TAF).

Aircraft registration & flight tracking

  • FAA N-Number Lookup — Search the U.S. civil aircraft registry by registration number, owner, manufacturer, model, or serial number.
  • Flightradar24 — Track live air traffic and review flight and aircraft histories worldwide.
  • LiveATC.net — Listen to live and archived air traffic control audio from airports worldwide.

Knowledge testing & study portals

  • FAA Knowledge Testing Through PSI — Schedule and manage an FAA airman knowledge test, including the Private Pilot Airplane test.
  • Federal Aviation Regulations (eCFR Title 14) — Read the current, continuously updated federal aviation regulations.
  • FAA Aviation Handbooks and Manuals — Access FAA pilot handbooks, flying handbooks, weather references, and other aviation manuals.
  • Airman Certification Standards (ACS) — Access the standards used for FAA knowledge testing, risk-management evaluation, and practical tests.
  • FAASafety.gov and WINGS — Take FAA safety courses, attend seminars, and participate in the WINGS Pilot Proficiency Program.

Flight-training operations

  • FlightCircle — Schedule aircraft and instructors and access flight-school or flying-club management functions.
  • Sporty’s — Online pilot training courses, test prep, and aviation gear from Sporty’s Pilot Shop.

Pilot organizations

  • Aircraft Owners and Pilots Association (AOPA) — General aviation advocacy, training resources, flight-planning tools, publications, and pilot services.
  • Experimental Aircraft Association (EAA) — Pilot education, homebuilt-aircraft resources, local chapters, Young Eagles, proficiency programs, and AirVenture.

Publications

Certification standards & testing

  • FAA-G-ACS-2 — ACS Companion Guide for Pilots — Explains the ACS framework, codes, testing procedures, and supporting guidance.
  • FAA-S-ACS-6C — Private Pilot for Airplane Category — Defines the knowledge, risk-management, and flight-proficiency standards for the private-pilot airplane practical test.
  • ACS Frequently Asked Questions — Answers common questions about ACS development, codes, knowledge testing, and practical-test use.
  • FAA-CT-8080-2H — Airman Knowledge Testing Supplement — Contains figures, charts, maps, tables, and performance information used during the private-pilot knowledge test.

Core pilot handbooks

  • FAA-H-8083-1B — Aircraft Weight and Balance Handbook — Explains aircraft weight-and-balance terminology, calculations, loading, center of gravity, and performance effects.
  • FAA-H-8083-2A — Risk Management Handbook — Covers aeronautical decision-making, hazard identification, risk assessment, and risk mitigation.
  • FAA-H-8083-3C — Airplane Flying Handbook — Explains airplane maneuvers, procedures, flight techniques, and common pilot errors.
  • FAA-H-8083-25C — Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge — The FAA's primary ground-study textbook covering aerodynamics, aircraft systems, weather, navigation, performance, and operations.
  • FAA-H-8083-28B — Aviation Weather Handbook — Comprehensive FAA reference covering aviation weather theory, hazards, weather products, and weather-related decision-making.
  • FAA-P-8740-2 — Density Altitude — FAA safety publication explaining density altitude and its effect on aircraft and engine performance.

Charts, procedures & terminology

  • Aeronautical Chart User's Guide — Explains FAA chart symbols, colors, airspace depictions, airport data, obstacles, and chart conventions.
  • Aeronautical Information Manual (AIM) — FAA guide to flight information, operating practices, air traffic control procedures, and the National Airspace System.
  • Pilot/Controller Glossary — Defines standardized terminology and phraseology used in pilot-controller communications.
  • NATO Phonetic Alphabet — The Alfa-through-Zulu spelling alphabet used in aviation radio communications.

Advisory circulars

  • AC 61-65K — Certification: Pilots and Flight and Ground Instructors — Provides certification guidance, knowledge-test procedures, and recommended sample logbook endorsements.
  • AC 90-66C — Non-Towered Airport Flight Operations — Provides recommended operating and communication procedures at airports without operating control towers.
  • AC 91-73B — Parts 91 and 135 Single Pilot, Flight School Procedures During Taxi Operations — Recommended standard operating procedures for taxi operations, aimed at preventing runway incursions.

FAA orders

  • JO 7110.65BB — Air Traffic Control — Prescribes air traffic control procedures, responsibilities, separation standards, and phraseology used by controllers.
  • JO 7900.5E — Surface Weather Observing — Prescribes procedures for aviation surface weather observations, including METAR and SPECI coding.
