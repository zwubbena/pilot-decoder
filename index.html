Resources

Certification, medical & airman records

  • Integrated Airman Certification and Rating Application (IACRA) — Apply for student pilot certificates, airman certificates, ratings, and other certification actions.
  • FAA MedXPress — Complete the online application for an FAA medical certificate before visiting an Aviation Medical Examiner.
  • Aviation Medical Examiner Locator — Find an FAA-designated Aviation Medical Examiner.
  • FAA Airmen Inquiry — Look up airman information and access FAA airman-record services.
  • FCC Universal Licensing System — Apply for and manage FCC radio licenses, including a Restricted Radiotelephone Operator Permit when required.

Weather & flight planning

  • Aviation Weather Center — Access METARs, TAFs, PIREPs, AIRMETs, SIGMETs, graphical forecasts, radar, satellite imagery, and other aviation weather products.
  • Leidos Flight Service (1-800-WX-BRIEF) — Obtain official weather briefings and file, amend, activate, or close flight plans.
  • SkyVector — View interactive VFR and IFR charts and use chart-based flight-planning tools.

Airports, NOTAMs & airspace

  • FAA Airport Data and Information Portal (ADIP) — Search official airport, runway, frequency, facility, ownership, and field-elevation data.
    • ADIP — Lockhart Municipal Airport (50R)
    • ADIP — Georgetown Executive Airport at Johnny Gantt Field (KGTU)
    • ADIP — Austin–Bergstrom International Airport (KAUS)
    • ADIP — San Marcos Regional Airport (KHYI)
  • FAA Airport Diagrams — Search and download official FAA airport diagrams by airport identifier.
  • FAA NOTAM Search — Search current Notices to Air Missions. Enter 50R for Lockhart Municipal Airport.
  • FAA Temporary Flight Restriction List — Review Temporary Flight Restrictions currently published by the FAA.
  • FAA VFR Raster Charts — Download official FAA sectional charts, terminal area charts, and other VFR chart products.
  • VFRMap — Pan and zoom the VFR sectional chart in your browser; search an identifier such as KGTU to jump to an airport.
  • FAA Chart Supplement — Download the Chart Supplement (formerly the Airport/Facility Directory) with airport, frequency, runway, and service details.
  • Terminal Area Forecast (TAF) — Query the FAA's historical and forecast aviation-activity data for U.S. airports (airport planning; distinct from the weather TAF).

Aircraft registration & flight tracking

  • FAA N-Number Lookup — Search the U.S. civil aircraft registry by registration number, owner, manufacturer, model, or serial number.
  • Flightradar24 — Track live air traffic and review flight and aircraft histories worldwide.
  • LiveATC.net — Listen to live and archived air traffic control audio from airports worldwide.

Knowledge testing & study portals

  • FAA Knowledge Testing Through PSI — Schedule and manage an FAA airman knowledge test, including the Private Pilot Airplane test.
  • Federal Aviation Regulations (eCFR Title 14) — Read the current, continuously updated federal aviation regulations.
  • FAA Aviation Handbooks and Manuals — Access FAA pilot handbooks, flying handbooks, weather references, and other aviation manuals.
  • Airman Certification Standards (ACS) — Access the standards used for FAA knowledge testing, risk-management evaluation, and practical tests.
  • FAASafety.gov and WINGS — Take FAA safety courses, attend seminars, and participate in the WINGS Pilot Proficiency Program.

Flight-training operations

  • FlightCircle — Schedule aircraft and instructors and access flight-school or flying-club management functions.
  • Sporty’s — Online pilot training courses, test prep, and aviation gear from Sporty’s Pilot Shop.

Pilot organizations

  • Aircraft Owners and Pilots Association (AOPA) — General aviation advocacy, training resources, flight-planning tools, publications, and pilot services.
  • Experimental Aircraft Association (EAA) — Pilot education, homebuilt-aircraft resources, local chapters, Young Eagles, proficiency programs, and AirVenture.
