PilotDECODER is my personal aviation learning tool. I'm Dr. Zane Wubbena, currently the Managing Director of Forecasting in School Finance at the Texas Education Agency. But I like airplanes, aviation, and learning.

Pilot Tools

  • METAR Decoder

    Any METAR (Meteorological Aerodrome Report), decoded into plain-English weather.

  • Density Altitude Decoder

    Density altitude worked line by line, the way the FAA written test does it.

  • Wind Tunnel Decoder

    Watch air flow over a wing and then change the angle of attack (AOA), see lift, drag, and stall live.
