PilotDECODER: Free Pilot Tools

PilotDECODER is my personal aviation learning tool. Maybe it can be yours, too. I'm Dr. Zane Wubbena, currently the Managing Director of Forecasting in School Finance at the Texas Education Agency. But I like airplanes, aviation, and learning.

Pilot Tools

  • METAR Decoder

    Decode any METAR (Meteorological Aerodrome Report) into plain-English aviation weather, with wind rose and thermometer visualizations. Works for any airport worldwide.

  • Density Altitude Calculator

    Learn how to calculate density altitude from pressure altitude and temperature, with every line of arithmetic shown and your result plotted on the chart, the way the FAA written test works it.

Resources

  • Aviation Weather Center · Current METARs, TAFs, and forecasts from the National Weather Service.
  • 1800WXBrief (Flight Service) · Official weather briefings and flight plan filing from Leidos Flight Service.
  • FAA Airport Data and Information Portal (ADIP) · Official field elevations, runways, and airport details.
  • FAA N-Number Lookup · Search the aircraft registry by registration number.
  • FAA Testing Supplement (FAA-CT-8080-2H) · Every figure used on the knowledge test, including the density altitude chart.
  • Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (PHAK) · The FAA's free ground school textbook.
  • IACRA · Integrated Airman Certification and Rating Application: student pilot and rating applications.
  • FAA MedXPress · Online application for the FAA medical certificate.
  • FAA BasicMed · Fly under BasicMed instead of a traditional medical certificate.
  • PSI FAA Testing Portal · Schedule the Private Pilot (PAR) knowledge test.
  • FCC Universal Licensing System · Restricted Radiotelephone Operator Permit and other radio licenses.
  • FAASafety.gov (WINGS) · FAA safety courses and the WINGS pilot proficiency program.
  • Flightradar24 · Live flight tracking worldwide.
