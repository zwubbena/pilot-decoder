Types identified in your photo appear in bold. The tool identifies eleven cloud types: the ten WMO standard cloud genera plus Towering Cumulus. Towering Cumulus is a species of Cumulus (cumulus congestus, where “congestus” is Latin for “piled up”), listed separately because it's the cue a fair-weather cloud is becoming a thunderstorm.

References:
  • World Meteorological Organization (WMO), International Cloud Atlas. Cloud identifications on this page are classified against its standard genera.
  • Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), FAA-H-8083-28, Aviation Weather Handbook. Cloud classification, the ten standard genera, and towering cumulus as a convective-hazard indicator. (Supersedes the now-canceled AC 00-6B.)
  • Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (PHAK), Aviation Weather chapter.
